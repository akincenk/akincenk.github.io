About

Operations experience, engineering execution

Most engineers have never run a shift. Most managers can't ship code. I do both.

I built my career in large-scale customer operations at Turkcell Global Bilgi, Turkcell's customer-experience company, and KKTCELL in Northern Cyprus. I led teams of 100–150 people across two countries. That meant hiring, training, scheduling, quality, KPIs and the daily job of keeping service levels up while costs stay down.

Running those operations showed me how much time goes into manual, repetitive work. So I learned to automate it. Today I build backend services, data pipelines and AI agents in Python. Recent work includes inventory and purchase-order systems, demand forecasting and staffing optimization, OCR analytics, and unattended monitoring services that run around the clock.

I'm based in Vancouver, BC. I'm looking for a role where both skill sets count: operations or team leadership, warehouse and logistics, or a technical role close to the business.
